Privacy Policy

GameWhame is operated by Ignia. This page explains privacy practices that are specific to the GameWhame website. It should be read together with Ignia's company privacy policy available at ignia.cc/privacy-policy.

Information we process

When you use GameWhame, technical information may be processed to deliver, protect and improve the service. This may include IP address, browser and device information, referring and requested URLs, approximate location derived from IP address, timestamps, diagnostic information and security logs.

GameWhame may also use browser storage, including cookies or local storage, for functions such as remembering recently played games, user preferences, consent choices and other site functionality.

Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors use the website, which games and pages are popular, device characteristics, session activity and performance. Where applicable, analytics storage will be controlled by the user's consent choices.

Advertising and Google Ad Manager

GameWhame is advertising-supported and may use Google Ad Manager and related Google advertising technologies. Google and participating advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers and similar technologies to select, deliver, measure, limit the frequency of and report on advertising.

Depending on the user's location and consent choices, advertising may be personalised or non-personalised. Where required, a consent-management platform will be used before non-essential advertising or analytics technologies are activated.

Third-party games and services

Games and other embedded services may process technical information required to operate in the browser. Third-party services linked from GameWhame may have their own privacy practices, and users should review those policies where appropriate.

Children

GameWhame is intended as a general-audience browser gaming service and is not designed specifically for children. Parents and guardians should supervise younger users and review the games they access.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the website and associated information. Technical and operational records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for security, legal, accounting, analytics and service-operation purposes.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability of personal information. You may also be able to withdraw consent through the site's consent controls. Privacy requests can be made using the company contact channels published on ignia.cc.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, advertising, analytics or legal requirements change. The version published on this page is the version that applies to GameWhame.
