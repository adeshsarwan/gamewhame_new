About GameWhame

GameWhame is a browser gaming website operated by Ignia. We publish and curate HTML5 games that can be played directly in a modern web browser without installing an app.

Our goal is to make games easy to discover and enjoyable on desktop and mobile devices. Each published game has its own page with gameplay information, controls, tips and related recommendations so players can understand the game before and while they play.

GameWhame is supported by advertising. Advertising helps us keep the service available without charging players for access. We aim to place advertising in a way that does not obscure gameplay, imitate navigation or encourage accidental interaction.

For information about the company behind GameWhame, visit Ignia.
