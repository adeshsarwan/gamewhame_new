Retro Speed

Racing

About Retro Speed

Retro Speed is an arcade racing game with a bright retro presentation. Your aim is to survive the road, react to traffic and keep your run going long enough to build a strong score. It works especially well for short sessions because a new attempt starts quickly.

How to play

Steer using the game controls and watch the road ahead. Leave enough room to change direction before an obstacle reaches you.

Tips

Look farther ahead than your car. Early lane changes are safer than last-second corrections.
